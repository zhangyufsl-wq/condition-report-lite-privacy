Condition Report Lite Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Data handling

Object details, inspection records, photos, and generated reports are stored on your device. Condition Report Lite does not provide an account system or first-party cloud synchronization and does not send this content to servers operated by us.

Camera and photo library

Camera or photo-library access is requested only when you choose to take or select a photo for a condition report.

PDF export and sharing

PDF reports are passed to the iOS share sheet only when you choose to export or share them. You select the destination.

Deletion and device storage

You can delete an object and its associated inspection history from the app. Uninstalling the app may remove local records. Device-level backup behavior is controlled by Apple and your system settings.

This policy applies to Condition Report Lite version 1.0 and later unless a newer policy is published here.
